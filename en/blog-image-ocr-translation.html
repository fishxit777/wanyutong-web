OCR identification and translation reminder to check separately

Image text recognition, translation and abstract are different steps. When the quantity, date, unit or negative word cannot be automatically checked, the deeppl translation is still provided and the verification reminder is added, and the whole sentence will not be blocked just because of such doubts. The reminder is not confirmed that the error is turned over, and it is not a guarantee of accuracy; the system will not rewrite the numbers without authorization. Translations may still not be available when blank, invalid results, or if the tag cannot be restored safely. If the picture distinguishes 15 as 75, please re-shoot or enter the correct original text, and do not take repeated translations as corrective identification results.

Different ways to deal with class schedules, announcements and SOPs

Class Schedule: Reserve the column name, and then split the block

Cut according to a single week or department, and keep the name column, date column, class and vacation symbol description. After completion, confirm "who, which day, which class is different", and don't just check whether the text is correct or not.

Announcement: Conditions and deadlines cannot be separated

Keep titles, applicable objects, deadlines, contact windows and exceptions. When the announcement is too long and needs to be divided into a map, indicate the page order, and check whether there are continuation statements or notes on each page.

SOP: Steps to check with warnings

Compare the steps number, action, machine code, usage and stop conditions. The small characters, arrows and pictures next to the picture are not pure text, and cannot be regarded as non-existent because OCR does not return; the safety instructions are still subject to the confirmation of the official document and the person in charge.
