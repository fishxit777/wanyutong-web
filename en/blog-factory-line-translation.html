A handover order, you must be able to retrieve the original work order

The squad leader fetches the handover field from the work order or point meter on the duty. A message is processed on a work order, and the other column is the number of completed, pending processing and pending judgments. Different batches of the same material number should be recorded separately, and the item number, batch number and work order number should be kept as they are. When encountering "two boxes" and other units, the quantity of each box should be specified according to the actual label, and the common packaging volume cannot be guessed.

From the sentence "12 left" to the explanation that can be taken over

The exercise template, the number and the information are all indications.

Handover work order WO-017, item number P-104, batch number L-03. The 28 piece has been completed, the 12 piece has been received at the point, and it is located at B-02. The successor 15:30 checks the quantity according to the point material meter, please return the squad leader if there is a difference.

Ask the successor to recount the results of his verification, such as "I will receive p-104, l- at the point 15:30 to b-02 03 12 pieces." If you see the 12 group instead of 12 For documents, first check the label and work order, and then correct it by the issuer. The correction information should be accompanied by the work order number and correction field, so that the next class knows which one to use.

Who are the materials, quality and equipment abnormalities?

  • Inconsistent material quantity or label. Return the warehouse management or squad leader. Record the expected value and point receipt value, and wait for the designated personnel to check and then update the shift order.
  • Batches to be judged by quality control. According to the process in the factory, hand it over to the quality control, and bring the batch number and disposal status when handing over the shift. The "seeing" in the translation cannot be regarded as approved release.
  • Involved equipment safety or personnel danger. Immediately use the in-plant official notification and on-site procedures. The LINE group can supplement the confirmed handover information, and the emergency processing should be in accordance with the instructions of the on-site personnel with the responsibility.

Avoid using the same "Okay" to indicate received, clicked or released. The succession table can be divided into three columns to be taken over, reviewed, and closed, and the record of the actual judge is retained.

  2. change a number
