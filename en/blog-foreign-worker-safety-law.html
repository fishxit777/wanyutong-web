Communication between foreign employees' work safety: use multi-language to explain risks, stop conditions and understanding confirmation

Write the work area, restrictions and contacts together before the class

The same sentence "Be careful today" may refer to different things in different teams. First, people who know the scene will write the original text, compare the on-site indication and approved operation methods, and let each language version refer to the same regional code, date, version and contact information. The group announcement can be used to remind that the leader should use the on-site explanation or the existing notification channel to send the message to each affected person.

Before translation, replace the words that depend on the context, such as "that side", "wait", and "turn it off" with a clear reference. After the translation, those who understand the language and are familiar with the work will check, and specifically confirm the prohibition and permission, sequence, number, unit and regional code. When the two versions are in and out, first process the existing procedures on the spot and find someone to clarify.

Replicable pre-shift explanation and reply template

Give this blank sample to the person in charge of the class to fill in, and then organize it according to the language of the members. Stop conditions, rendezvous and resumption methods must be taken from the approved process on site.

Multilingual questions that can be used for drills

  • Traditional Chinese Please use your own words to explain: What should I stop? Who do you want to contact?
  • Please explain in your own words: When should you stop work, and whom should you contact?
  • Hãy giải thích bằng lời của bạn: Khi nào bạn cần dừng công việc và bạn sẽ liên hệ với ai?

This group of questions can be used to practice two-way communication. Before the actual class, the description needs to be reviewed according to the content of the work and the language of the members, and those who are familiar with the scene will review it.

Separate "received" and "know what to do"

  1. Ask the staff to point out the location

    Confirm the work area with a field map or a sign visible in a safe location. Region nicknames may be inconsistent in different languages, it is best to keep the official brand in the original and each translation.

  2. Ask the staff to explain in their own words

    Don't just ask "do you understand". Use the drill situation and ask "If you walk to the affected area along the original route, who will you find first?" Then ask the other party to repeat the stop condition. You can answer in familiar language, and arrange artificial interpreting if necessary.

  3. Make up for those who lack replies

    The shifter records members who have not yet been contacted, especially late arrivals, shifts, temporary or temporary support personnel. When there are still questions at the scene, first use the existing program to confirm, and do not regard group reading or stickers as an understanding.

For example, the reply in the exercise is "I go to the original entrance, etc.", but the on-duty announcement refers to "the original entrance has been suspended". The explainer can point out the original entrance and temporary channel on the map, and then ask the other party to re-explain, and add the brands of the two entrances into the multilingual announcement.

When changing shifts, changing routes or emergencies, take the on-site notification

  • Plan changes:Re-release a single valid version, indicating the start time, the change item and the old version are invalid. Synchronize new notifications to successors and temporary support, and re-confirm key matters.
  • Translation is ambiguous:Give the original text and ambiguous sentences to those who are familiar with the scene and the language, and first clarify the requirements. If the permission and prohibition of the two languages are contradictory, they will be notified through the original on-site channel immediately, so that no one can guess by themselves.
  • Immediate danger:Report and evacuate according to the on-site emergency procedures. In case of fire, emergency assistance or emergency ambulance, you can call 119. When reporting the case, follow the instructions of the wiring personnel to indicate the location, condition and whether the person is trapped.The fire department of the Ministry of the Interior reported the essentials of the case

Emergency events are not equal to group translation.Let each class know in advance the contact information that can also be used offline. Chat translation is suitable for helping to understand the approved text, and formal on-site instructions, resumption judgment, training and adaptation are handled according to the procedures of each workplace.
