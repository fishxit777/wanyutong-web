Confirm one thing before starting: The bot message in the LINE group is displayed to all members of the group. WanyuTong does not secretly display different content for different members in the same group, and does not write "visible group" as a personal private message.

The group message sent by bot, all members can see it

The group description of the LINE Developers states that the message that the bot sends in the group or multi-person chat room is displayed to all members. WanyuTong first displays the reproducible main language of the first specified target, and other languages are viewed one by one with buttons; the clicked translation will still appear in the same group in public.

Don't take the multilingual button as a secret diversionIf the content includes salary, medical records, personnel sanctions, identity documents or other sensitive materials, you should first confirm whether it is suitable for the entire group, and switch to the official one-to-one channel if necessary. Dynamic results cache 24 hours, the button will not re-translate or deduct the quota; at present, the bot does not provide the withdrawal or in-situ collection of the sent translation.

Seven-step drill before officially joining the working group

  1. Check the instructions in the personal dialogue

    Send first @新手教學 Either @說明, confirm that the bot can reply normally.

  2. List the languages you really need right now

    Only join the language used by existing members to avoid unnecessary multi-language messages.

  3. First test a set of two-way translation

    Set traditional Chinese and a target language, and first confirm that both directions can be understood.

  4. Invite BOT to join the test group

    Don't use formal customers or high-risk job content from the start.

  5. Measurement time, quantity and negative words

    For example, "9 month 1 day 08:00 wear two pairs of gloves, do not enter the B area".

  6. Please repeat the target language user

    Ask the other party to reply to the time, quantity, location and prohibition, not only the reply map or "understand".

  7. Record failure conditions

    If the proper noun, code or context is prone to errors, establish a fixed bilingual vocabulary and ask the supervisor to review.
