The most common problem for the first setting is not a functional failure, but a certain step less: only add friends but do not view the instructions, there is no test after setting the language, or the bot is put into the group and directly tested with important information. A relatively stable approach is to first complete the test with a short sentence that does not involve name, amount and industrial safety.

Prepare three things before starting

  • Confirm that the WanyuTong official LINE account is added, and the LINE ID is @969wpxno。
  • Determine which languages the group mainly needs, such as Traditional Chinese, Indonesian, Vietnamese or Filipino.
  • Prepare two test texts: one daily notice, one simple reply. Formal work safety, medical or payment contents are left to use after the test is passed.
Safety reminderTranslation can reduce misunderstandings, but when it comes to industrial safety, medical care, law, salary and amount, it should still be re-confirmed by those who know the situation.

FAQ

Why is there no automatic translation after joining a friend?

Send first @說明 And @語言, confirm the current mode and language settings, and then enter the general text test. If there is still no reply, please keep the screen and ask through the official customer service form.

Can I use the work safety or payment information test from the beginning?

Not recommended. Confirm the process with daily sentences without risks; content involving safety, medical treatment, law or amount must be manually reconfirmed.
