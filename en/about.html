About WanyuTong

Leave translations, abstracts and work records in the same LINE process

WanyuTong is a LINE auxiliary tool for Taiwan's cross-language team. The supervisor can be assigned in the group, the BOT outputs and translates according to the settings, and the team will use the reply, the summary and the report to leave a record that can be checked back.

The scope of operation and management currently provided

The private message of the mobile phone can be directly clicked on the bottom menu; the LINE platform does not support group fixed bottom graphic menu, please send pure text @選單 in the group, and open the same group of commonly used function cards. At present, there is no list of automatic confirmation return, read or not responded to person by person; The abstract only organizes the recorded content, and the actual number of the actual arrangements shall prevail.

Products

WanyuTong currently takes LINE Bot as the main service portal, providing text translation, speech transcription and translation, pictures OCR , group multilingual output, abstract, report, record search and statistics. The actual available functions are determined according to the plan, group opening status and third-party service status.

The group multilingual mode is not to display different messages to different members in private. After the supervisor sends a sentence, the first designated target is displayed in the main language that can be copied first, and the other languages are viewed one by one by buttons. Dynamic results cache 24 hours, the button will not re-translate or deduct the quota; the clicked translation will still be disclosed to the whole group, and the LINE message cannot be recovered or folded.

Translation with AI limitations

Machine translation, speech recognition, OCR and abstracts may all be affected by accent, background noise, image quality, proper noun or context. High-risk content such as work safety, medical care, amount, law, contract and personnel punishment should be re-confirmed by those who are qualified or on the scene, and should not only rely on the results of the bot to make the final decision.

Users can refer toTranslation quality inspectionAndOCR shooting checklist, reduce common errors.
