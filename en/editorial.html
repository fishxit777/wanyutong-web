WanyuTong Content Editing and Testing Principles

This page describes how to generate, verify, update and correct the product operation, work guide and plan data on the official website. The goal is to make the reader clear: which is the actual product process, which are general work suggestions, and which content must be re-confirmed by the person.

The WanyuTong official website also includes product introduction, operation teaching and cross-language work management suggestions. The evidence requirements of the three contents are different: the picture is a schematic diagram, and the update date of the document is not equal to the test day of the real machine, and the program check is not equal to the official service is available at the moment. The articles indicate the source, scope of application, historical tests and known limitations, and do not write speculation as a proven function, and do not add persuasiveness with fictional cases or unreconciled evaluations.

Who is responsible for public content

The content of the website is maintained by the WanyuTong team. The product name, instruction, plan, language quantity and functional scope in the article should be consistent with the official website plan page, the current reply and the actual reproducible process of LINE Bot.

The public signature uses the "WanyuTong Team" and does not disclose private names. If you need to report an error, you can useOfficial contact pageOr the customer service form, and attach the page URL, wrong paragraph and reproducible screen.

Test method for product operation articles

The following are the standards for implementing the actual machine test, which does not mean that each file modification has been retested. When there is no new actual machine record, the article only writes the method/document verification method, and keeps the historical evidence date.

  1. Confirm entry and version

    Starting with the WanyuTong official LINE entrance, it is confirmed that the test is currently public bots, not old screenshots, test branches or other services.

  2. Use low-risk test sentences

    First test the general collection notice, and then measure the time, quantity, negative words, location and code name respectively. The test content does not contain real customers, credit cards, ID cards, medical records or private group information.

  3. Check forward and reverse processes

    The original text of the test supervisor is translated to the target language, and it also tests whether the employee can turn back to traditional Chinese after replying in the target language.

  4. Record limitations and labor responsibility

    If the results may be affected by the accent, picture quality, proper noun or context, the article must explain; work safety, medical, legal and amount content must be separately marked with manual review.

External Data and Reference Principles

The product function is mainly evidenced by the actual process of WanyuTong. When involved with a third-party platform or professional field, give priority to original or official sources, such as:

  • LINE Developers: Rich Menu (mobile phone private message and group restriction)
  • LINE Developers: Group and Multiplayer Messaging API
  • DeepL Developers: Support Language
  • Department of Occupational Safety and Health, Ministry of Labor
  • National Regulatory Database

If the data may change with the version, policy or plan, the article will attach a check date, and remind readers to serve the current screen or the latest announcement of the competent authority.

How to fix after finding errors

  • Check the original evidence first; if necessary, reproduce the problem, and do not directly guess the cause according to a single screenshot.
  • Synchronously check the home page, functions, charges, FAQ, start use, related articles and structured data.
  • After the substantive correction, update the file modification date and Sitemap; the actual machine date is additionally listed, not only to change the date and pretend to complete the actual measurement.
  • If the major function changes have not been deployed, the planning content will not be written as an available function in advance; highly repeated articles are merged, and the old entry is reserved.

Content and advertising are separated

The WanyuTong product and service are separated from the official website content-based advertising. Website advertisements do not affect the free quota, paid activation, translation sorting or customer service processing of LINE Bot. Advertisements are only planned and placed on the knowledge base and article pages with complete content, not on join, payment, login, privacy or pure guide screens.
