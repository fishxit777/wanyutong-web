How to choose the three common needs?

  1. I don't know if it is suitable for:First use the free version to complete a set of two-way text translation.
  2. Requires photos, voice or reports:First use the monthly fee version to test 30 days, and then change the long-term plan after confirming the workflow.
  3. Group use fixed daily:Compare the half-year version and the one-year version, not just the single payment amount.

Don't buy a long-term plan directly for a few occasional messages, and don't use free text translation when you clearly need OCR or reports. The plan should match the work needs, not the other way around.
