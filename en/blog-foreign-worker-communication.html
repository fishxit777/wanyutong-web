Cross-language workflow

Communication of foreign employees: separate work notices, abnormal responses from rights and interests

Translation tools are suitable for assisting daily notifications and responses, but should not intercept or replace formal complaints, medical, police, occupational safety and labor rights channels. A good process will let employees know: general questions go back to the group, which is the official entrance for sensitive or urgent questions.

The notification confirms that the team will take the initiative to reply

WanyuTong does not currently provide automatic confirmation return, read by person or not responded to the list. Please check the response list by yourself, and members should reply and reiterate the key points in their own language; do not regard the read or abstract of LINE as proof of understanding and completion. Group transmission @選單 can enable common functions such as language settings.

"Reading" does not mean "understanding", and "translation" does not mean "problem has been solved". The first step in cross-language management is not to add more information, but to fix the fields that each message should contain, who is responsible for replying, and what situation must be upgraded.

Establish a four-tier communication process

  1. general notice

    Collection, scheduling, items and daily assignments are used in a fixed format, and the translation and reply are completed in the group.

  2. need to be clarified

    Employees can use their own language to point out sentences that they do not understand, and the supervisor can rewrite it into shorter and more specific content.

  3. The scene is abnormal

    The equipment, injury, quality or accommodation problem use the "location + problem + immediate danger" format, and hand it over to the designated person in charge for handling.

  4. Equity and Emergency Help

    When involving complaints, personal safety, labor disputes or other sensitive matters, provide a formal and confidential competent authority or emergency liaison channel, and do not require only report in the company group.

First make the original text easy to understand, and the translation has a higher chance to retain the correct meaning. Machines, chemicals, industrial safety and medical terms should establish a fixed vocabulary that has been manually confirmed.

There are four types of employee replies, not just "received"

  • Confirm:Received + restoration time/place/quantity.
  • Not clear:Point out which sentence or which word does not understand.
  • Unable to cooperate:Explain the reason and the time that can be coordinated, and do not require the disclosure of medical privacy.
  • Immediate exception:Location + problem + whether someone is injured or is still in danger.
Emergency don't wait for botPersonal safety, fire, serious injuries or dangers that are occurring, should follow the on-site emergency procedures and contact appropriate emergency services; translation tools are not an emergency notification system.

In case of labor rights, provide the official 1955 channel

The Ministry of Labor Foreigners' Labor Rights Network explained that 1955 is a national labor consultation and appeal line, providing multilingual services, legal consultation, appeal, legal assistance information and referral services and other services. Such problems should not be left only within the employer-managed workgroup.

  • Labor Development Department of the Ministry of Labor: 1955 Labor Consulting and Complaint Line
  • Foreigners' Labor Rights and Interests Network
  • Department of Occupational Safety and Health, Ministry of Labor

This article only provides general process arrangement; individual cases, appeal timeliness, legal responsibility and handling methods, subject to the latest explanations of the competent authority or qualified professionals.

Workgroups are not suitable for carrying all sensitive issues

Messages in the LINE group will be seen by members. Medical records, passports, residence permits, complete banking information, sexual harassment complaints, personnel sanctions or other sensitive content should use formal channels with clear authority, confidentiality and handling responsibilities.

Managers can publicly "how to ask for help", and should not require employees to disclose all evidence and personal information in all groups. Please refer to the website data processing instructionsPrivacy Policy。
