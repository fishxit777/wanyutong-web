Privacy Policy

WanyuTong is a multilingual communication, translation, summarization, and recordkeeping tool for LINE work groups. This policy explains how we may handle data to provide the service, maintain system security, process payments, offer customer support, and monetize the website through advertising.

Last updated: 2026-11-10

1. Data we may collect

To provide our LINE Bot and website services, we may process LINE user IDs, group IDs, language settings, translation counts, expiration status, payment and activation records, necessary system and error logs, website browsing records, and information users voluntarily provide for support.

Our first-party website analytics record only the public page name and referring domain in the page and referral fields. We do not retain the path or query parameters of a referring URL or use private source text submitted for translation to track website use. Legacy first-party traffic records include browser information, source IP address, and time. New page-view and LINE link-click events do not store IP addresses, browser information, or visitor identifiers. Hosting providers and security systems may still process IP addresses. Hosting or other providers may also process related technical logs to establish connections, maintain security, and provide services.

The new website analytics count visits to each public page separately from clicks on links to the official WanyuTong LINE account. These events carry no LINE member IDs, chat content, or visitor-identifying cookies. Each event receives a unique ID to avoid duplicate counting. Click counts do not indicate that someone added the account as a friend or represent the number of unique visitors.

2. Translation and message processing

When you use text translation, image OCR, speech-to-text, or summarization in WanyuTong, relevant content may be sent to LINE, DeepL, OpenAI, Anthropic, or other necessary service providers depending on the feature. See the section below for the text-based risk checks and notification scope. We do not make additional paid image OCR calls for security scans. Users should manually verify important medical, legal, financial, contractual, and occupational safety content.

3. Why we use data

  • Provide translation, OCR, speech, summarization, reporting, search, and group management features.
  • Handle payments, activation, payment adjustments, reconciliation, and customer support.
  • Maintain system stability, perform security audits, troubleshoot errors, and detect risks of fraud and abuse.
  • Analyze website traffic, improve content, and measure advertising performance.

4. Chat safety risk detection

Content risk rules check the text and links actually sent to the Bot. If a user requests image translation, the rules may reuse text already extracted by that feature. Security checks do not make additional paid OCR calls. Images without extracted text cannot be treated as security-scanned. WanyuTong does not read other LINE conversations the Bot has not joined or received.

Events that meet the notification threshold are sent to the sole private administrator for manual review, using only a fixed risk category, an incident reference, and recommended action. We do not forward customer source text or free-form summaries. Low-risk signals may be kept only in audit logs. Content concerns and platform sleep, quota, or delay are yellow alerts and do not establish an intrusion. Red alerts are reserved for actual system security incidents. An advance warning does not establish unlawful conduct or a crime and does not automatically trigger a public accusation or user block. Delivery depends on host and channel availability.

5. Website advertising and cookies

Advertising is currently disabled. The website retains its AdSense ownership verification tag and ads.txt file. The tag does not load ads or show that Google has approved the website. Ads on content pages will run only after the site is approved and delivery settings and applicable privacy consent measures are in place.

If Google AdSense is enabled, Google and participating providers may use advertising cookies to select ads based on visits to this site and other websites. Ad requests may also involve browser or device information, web beacons, IP addresses, and other identifiers for delivery, measurement, or abuse prevention. See How Google uses information from partner sites。

  • Visit Google Ads Settings to manage or disable personalized ads.
  • If other participating providers offer an opt-out, use the third-party personalized ad choices tool to manage it. Availability depends on the provider and browser.
  • Your browser also offers cookie controls. Turning off personalization may not remove all ads or automatically erase existing service records.

This policy does not replace a consent management mechanism. Before delivering personalized advertising to visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, we must implement the certified CMP required by Google and applicable consent settings. This policy does not count as visitor consent. No other advertising providers were enabled in this policy update.

We do not sell LINE group conversations to advertisers or make private translation content publicly available to third-party advertisers.

6. Third-party services

WanyuTong may use LINE, DeepL, OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, GitHub Pages, Render, ECPay, and other cloud or payment services. Each provider has its own privacy policy and data processing rules. Users should also read their official information.

7. Data retention and deletion

For recent translation history, we currently retain the latest 100 entries per source for lookup. Each lookup displays 20 entries by default. These are different limits and do not establish a universal retention period. Payment, support, and security audit records are handled separately according to their necessary purposes.

The system may retain recent translation, usage, and payment records for history lookup, usage statistics, debugging, customer support, accounting, and legal compliance. To access, correct, or delete data, contact us through the Google support form or email us at wanyutong29@gmail.com. Records required for legal, accounting, audit, or security purposes may not be deleted immediately.

8. Children and sensitive data

This service is intended primarily for employers, managers, and work teams rather than children. Do not send sensitive data that is not needed, such as entire identity documents, medical diagnoses, bank account numbers, passwords, or full confidential contracts.

9. Policy updates

We may update this policy as features, regulations, advertising services, or third-party services change. Where possible, we will announce significant changes on our website or through the LINE Bot.
