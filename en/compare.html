Presets for three requirements

  1. I just want to understand a sentence quickly

    You can use the familiar general translation tools first, and you don't have to import the group system for a one-time message.

  2. Assign, reply and check back in the LINE group every day

    First use WanyuTong to test the group process with free text translation; voice and pictures are required for OCR, abstract, report or record functions, and then evaluate the paid group plan.

  3. Contract, medical, legal or major work safety content

    Prioritize the search for professional interpreters/interpretations and responsible persons who are qualified or familiar with the field, and machine translation can only be used as an auxiliary.
