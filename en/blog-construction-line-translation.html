Multilingual assignment at the construction site: work surface, change notice and on-site confirmation

Multi-working shifts are most often read in different versions on the floor, entrance and reschedule notice. Write the work surface that you can locate the identifiable version on the map, give the identifiable version to each change, and ask the foreman of each work to report where and when you will be present.

First write "That District" as a position you can find

The same construction site may have two stairs, multiple entrances, and there may also be the third floor and the third floor of the house. From the complete position of the approved picture or area identification, at least write the area, floor and area code, and then mark the entrance port that needs to be used. If there is a lack of consistent signs, the chief responsible person will first sort out and compare, and point out the location when assembling on the spot.

The change notice indicates which version is invalid

Schedule drill template, the location and time are all indications.

[Gathering Notice B2-017 V2] 11 / 21 10:00, East Side of Building B 02, gather at the east entrance. The original V1's 09:00 and the West have been voided. This rule only updates the collection time and entrance, and the foreman of each class is requested to reply to V2, time, and entrance.

When the date, area or entry point has changed, let the foreman see a new notification with the full field. In line with the on-site habits, put the valid version in the group announcement or schedule, and at the same time mark the old version to be invalid. If the foreman only returns the expression or "received", he will further ask him to recount the content of the change, and then update the attendance confirmation form of the team.

Confirmation of collection notice and on-site work separately

Multi-language messages can be transmitted to meet the meeting time, tool handover or schedule change. Involving the hoisting, control area, temporary electricity use, construction methods, safety measures or emergency situation, it shall be handled in accordance with on-site command, official drawings, approved plans and notification procedures. Persons who have the right to decide should confirm the understanding of the relevant personnel on the spot, and cannot use group translations, read or abstracts instead.

If the oral instructions and group text in the same district are contradictory, the foreman should directly find the person in charge to check the valid version. Only leave a message in the group and wait for the translation to delay the judgment, and the immediate danger should go to the existing emergency notification.

Do a reverse confirmation before the shift

  1. Schematic schedules that do not involve actual work first

    Select a fictional area, ask members of different languages to repeat the floor and entrance, and then check whether the location has been translated into another area name.

  2. Let one of the shifts receive the old version

    When the drill receives V1 and V2, where to find a valid version, and confirm that the notification person can check the work that has not been replied.
