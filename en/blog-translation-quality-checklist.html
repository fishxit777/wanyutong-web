Translation Quality Guide

The completion of translation does not mean the completion of the assignment: seven types of information require manual checking

The most dangerous mistakes in work translation are usually not the whole sentence is incomprehensible, but a "no", the time difference is one hour, the unit is ignored, or the machine code is regarded as ordinary text.

File update: 2026 - 11 - 10Reading time: about 8 minutesApplicable: factories, construction sites, care and catering

What should I do when I see a verification reminder?

When the quantity, date, unit or negative word cannot be automatically checked, the deeppl translation is still provided and the verification reminder is added, and the whole sentence will not be blocked just because of such doubts. The reminder is not confirmed that the error is turned over, and it is not a guarantee of accuracy; the system will not rewrite the numbers without authorization. Translations may still not be available when blank, invalid results, or if the tag cannot be restored safely. Please compare the following seven types of information item by item; the same sentence is written in different language orders, Chinese characters or dates in different languages, which does not mean that the value must be changed.

The translation engine can quickly process a lot of general text, but it does not know the nickname of the machine inside the team, who is today's foreman, and does not know whether "wait for it" is on the scene for five minutes or an hour. Quality management should start with the original text, plus the translation after translation and confirmation.

Write the original text clearly, and the translation will have the opportunity to be accurate

"Take that late, remember not to make mistakes" may be enough for those familiar with the scene, but there is a lack of time, items, location and judgment criteria for foreign employees. Can be changed to:

Clear versionBefore today 15:00, please move the 12 box parts in Area A to the second floor B line. The parts marked "A0 3" should not be moved. Please reply "Completed + Name" after completion.

Such original texts are easier to execute than vague sentences even without translation. After translation, the time, quantity, location and negation conditions can also be compared item by item.

Don't just ask "do you understand?"

The employee's reply to "understand" may just indicate that he sees the message, which does not necessarily mean that he understands every detail. The important assignment can ask the other party to repeat three key points in their own language:

  1. When will it be done before?
  2. What to do and how much?
  3. Which thing is forbidden to do?

The supervisor will look back at the traditional Chinese that has been turned back, and if all three items are correct, the confirmation will be completed. This is more misunderstanding than just reading or sticking.

Four categories of content cannot be only relying on automatic translation

  • Work safety:Shut down, power outages, high places, chemicals and emergency evacuation.
  • Medical:Drug name, dosage, allergy, symptoms and medical instructions.
  • Law:Contracts, rights and obligations, conditions for termination and compensation.
  • Amount:Salary, overtime, payment account number, exchange rate and tax.
Manual review is not an optionThe above content should be confirmed by the person with responsibilities and circumstances, and professional translation or interpretation is used if necessary.

Directly apply work notification formats

Subject: Notice of Equipment Downtime
Time: 2026 / 08 / 20 14:00 – 16:00
Location: A0 3 machine on the first floor
Action: Stop the operation and move to the B-line support
Prohibition: Do not restart until the repair is completed
Reply: received + name

The fixed format allows each translation to have the same field, which is also convenient for search and handover after that.
