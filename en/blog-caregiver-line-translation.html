Family care handover: daily arrangement, return and privacy

Family groups are suitable for arranging purchase, visit and daily handover. First, make an agreement with the caregiver who to take over, when to reply, and what matters to find family members or professionals directly, so as to avoid overwhelming important content in the chat.

First agree on daily life, sensitivity and urgent matter where to go

Family members may think that another person has taken over after seeing the message. It is recommended to separate the daily handover from the things that need to be handled privately. The family members and caregivers first confirm the main contact person with the backup, and agree who can reply and who will deal with the rescheduling in the group.

The daily necessities are handed over to a person, and the reply indicates that the project is not completed

In daily handover drills, all information is indicated.

Before 11 / 21 15:30, please put the 2 wrapping paper in the living room white cabinet. The morning shift carer confirms that if only the 1 package is left, please reply to the number, and the family is responsible for re-purchasing.

The takeover please reply in the habitual language "currently the 1 bag has been put in the white cabinet in the living room, and the 1 bag is still missing". After receiving it, the designated family members will explain who to make and when. Add a date to cross-heaven matters, so as to avoid reading "tomorrow" the next day as another day. Photographs can assist in contrasting items, but photos should avoid name, address and irrelevant private information.

Family groups may also contain people who don't need to know

Arrange the available date, time and receptionist of the visit, and there is no need to post the full address, identity information or medical record. Identifying medicine bags, transcribing professional descriptions or judging symptoms involves sensitive content and medical decision-making, and should be handled by appropriate professional channels. When the handwritten or photo is difficult to identify, go back to the original document to check with the medical staff, and cannot guess with the translation results.

Practice two daily messages before using

  1. Test dates and items

    The bilingual setting is tested in two different time housework tests. Ask the other party to recount the time, quantity and position, and then check the original text.

  2. Replies that cannot be completed by the test

    Taking "not enough items" as an example, confirm that the caregiver knows who to buy and whether the other party has taken over.

  3. Contact information when the translation expires

    If the group is delayed or the translation is unclear, confirm with the pre-agreed telephone or other available pipes. Medical consultation and emergency help maintain their official contact information.
